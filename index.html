Google Sheets: use File → Download → Excel (.xlsx) or CSV, then import it here.

How to play

  • Flip: tap the card, press Enter, or use the flip button.
  • Correct: swipe right-to-left, press →, or tap the green thumb.
  • Incorrect: swipe left-to-right, press ←, or tap the red thumb.
  • Card change: a thin light runs round the card border for half a second, then the whole card slides away.
  • Previous card: Undo button or Right Shift. Re-marking a card replaces its old result.
  • Long text: swipe, mouse wheel, or ↑ / ↓.
  • Images: + / − buttons or Ctrl + = / Ctrl + − to zoom; drag or Ctrl + arrows to pan; double-tap/double-click or Space for fullscreen; Esc or ✕ to close.
  • Timer: when on, each card gets the chosen time; if it runs out, the game moves on without recording an answer.
  • Randomize: shuffles cards on import; front and back stay paired.
  • Export / quit: the ☰ menu exports incorrect answers (Excel) or quits. You'll also be offered the export at the end.
