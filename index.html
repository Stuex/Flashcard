How to play

  • Import an .xlsx, .xls, .csv, or .pdf file. Column A is the front, column B is the back.
  • Thumbs up = right: swipe right to left, tap it, or press →.
  • Thumbs down = wrong: swipe left to right, tap it, or press ←.
  • Undo = previous card: tap it or press Right Shift.
  • Flip: tap the card, tap the button, or press Enter.
  • Long text: scroll with the mouse wheel, drag up or down, or press ↑ ↓.
  • Images: double tap or double click to open full screen. Zoom with Ctrl +/−, move with Ctrl + arrows or by dragging, close with Esc or ✕.
  • Timer: when it runs out, the game moves on to the next card.
  • At the end, or from the settings button, download an Excel file of your wrong cards.
