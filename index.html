How to Play Stuex Flashcard

1. Green Thumbs Up (👍): Marks right answer. Swipe Right-to-Left or press Right Arrow. Goes to next card.

2. Red Thumbs Down (👎): Marks wrong answer. Swipe Left-to-Right or press Left Arrow. Goes to next card.

3. Undo (↩): Returns to previous flashcard. Press Right Shift.

4. Flip Card (🔄): Flips card front to back. Single tap or press Enter.

Image Controls:
- Zoom In/Out: Ctrl + = / Ctrl + -
- Fullscreen: Double tap / Double left click or Enter
- Move Zoomed Image: Arrow keys or directional swipes.
