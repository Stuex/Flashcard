How to Play Stuex Flashcard

1. Importing Spreadsheets: Click "Import File" to load local `.xlsx`, `.xls`, or `.csv` files. Columns A (Front) and B (Back) are automatically mapped into flashcards across all worksheets.

2. Study Controls: Use the green thumbs-up (Right Arrow) for correct answers and the red thumbs-down (Left Arrow) for incorrect answers. Press Enter or click the card to flip between front and back.

3. Navigation & Undo: Use Right Shift to undo your last step. Swipe left/right on mobile or use mouse gestures.

4. Timers & Randomization: Enable the optional countdown timer or card randomization from the Main Page before starting.

5. Images & Text: Pinch, scroll long text, or click/double-tap images to view them in fullscreen with pan and zoom capabilities (Ctrl + / - to zoom, arrow keys to pan).

6. Exporting Incorrect Answers: Access the settings gear in the upper left during gameplay to export a customized Excel file (`wrong_[filename].xlsx`) containing only your red-button-tapped flashcards.
